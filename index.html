AureVon

A fashion storefront where you turn the garment in 3D before you buy, with the whole shop behind it: checkout, stock, couriers, returns and reports.

  • 3D product viewer built on three.js and React Three Fiber, with an asset pipeline that checks and compresses every model.
  • Typed end to end. The TypeScript API client is generated from the OpenAPI schema, and CI fails if they drift.

Ideal Future School

The public website of a real school in Nowshera Virkan, in English and Urdu, with online admissions and an office panel the staff run themselves.

  • Every page in both languages, with proper right-to-left Nastaliq, switched in one tap.
  • Admission requests online, tracked from new to admitted and exported to Excel.
  • Staff update it without a developer, with separate roles for principal, office and content.
  • Hardened: login lockout, idle sign-out, a security log and Content-Security-Policy headers.
