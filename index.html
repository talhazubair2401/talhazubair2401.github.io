Work

Two complete systems you can try right now. Both demos sign you in with one click and use made-up data, so press anything.

Nexora SafarPro

One system for the whole travel agency: bookings, tickets, visas, Umrah groups, tours and accounts. It works even when the internet doesn't.

Try the live demo See the code

The challenge

Agencies run on WhatsApp chats, registers and Excel. The owner can't quickly see who still owes money, which supplier is due this week, which pilgrims are missing passports, or whether the month made a profit.

What I built

  • Runs on the agency's own PC, fully offline, with one-click verified backups to a USB drive.
  • Receipts can't be quietly changed. Mistakes are reversed, never edited, so the books stay honest.
  • Umrah groups with every pilgrim's passport, visa, room and payment in one list.
  • Staff logins with limits. A receptionist can't see accounts or take payments.

The result

One dashboard shows sales, collections, overdue customers and supplier dues for every branch, with profit and loss calculated from the same records.

For developers: Python, Django and SQLite with vanilla JavaScript. A double-entry ledger engine, server-side permission checks on every action, protection against double submissions, and 39 automated tests.

Nexora School Office

Fees, receipts, attendance, results and certificates for a school office. First built for a real private school.

Try the live demo See the code

The challenge

Fees lived in a register, receipts were written by hand and certificates typed in Word. At month end nobody knew exactly who owed fees, and nothing stopped someone from quietly lowering a fee.

What I built

  • A month's fees for every student in one click, part payments, receipts and challans with every unpaid month.
  • Attendance and result cards with grade and class position worked out automatically.
  • Certificates in English and Urdu on the school letterhead.
  • Only the principal can change a fee, delete anything or see salaries and profit. Blocked attempts are logged.

The result

Receipts, challans, result cards and certificates print in seconds, and the principal sees who collected what and what the school earned. The demo uses a fictional school; the real school's records stay private.

For developers: Python, Django, SQLite, Bootstrap and Chart.js. Access rules enforced in middleware, transaction-safe serial numbers, print-ready layouts, and 56 automated tests.

How a project works

No jargon. You see working software early, and we change it until it fits how your office works.

  1. I look at your registers

    We sit together for an hour. I see how your office keeps records today and what slows you down.

  2. You try a demo

    You get a working demo shaped around your fees, bookings or stock, to click through with your staff before anything is final.

  3. I install and train

    The system goes on your office computer. I train your staff and move your existing records in.

  4. Backups and support

    Backups go to a USB drive every week, and I'm on WhatsApp when something needs changing.
